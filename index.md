# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Meta/Instagram application operated by Shubham ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you interact with Instagram or Facebook accounts connected to it.

## What the App does

The App is a personal automation tool. It receives webhook events from Meta's platforms (Instagram and Facebook) and processes them on a self-hosted n8n instance. It may also send notifications to a private Telegram chat controlled by the operator.

## Information we process

- Instagram/Facebook account IDs and usernames
- Comment, message, and mention text
- Media IDs and URLs included in webhook payloads
- Timestamps and event metadata
- Technical request data such as IP address and request headers

## How we use it

- To trigger automations configured by the operator
- To send notifications to the operator
- To debug and maintain the integration

## Storage and sharing

- Data is processed on a self-hosted server controlled by the operator.
- We do not sell, rent, or share data with advertisers.
- Data is shared only with service providers needed to operate the App: Meta (platform APIs), Cloudflare (network tunnel), and Telegram (notifications).
- Event payloads and logs are retained for up to 30 days and then deleted.

## Your choices and deletion

You can revoke the App's access at any time in your Instagram or Facebook settings under Apps and Websites. To request deletion of data processed by the App, email **shubhamv306@gmail.com**. We will delete the relevant records within 30 days.

## Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including HTTPS, webhook signature verification, and restricted access to the self-hosted infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes.
